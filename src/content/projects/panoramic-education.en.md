## Background

During a Beijing University of Technology university–industry internship at 北京伟开赛德科技发展有限公司, I helped improve a smart education website.

## My role and contribution

I integrated TensorFlow.js and BodyPix to implement browser-based human segmentation, background processing, and AI interaction for panoramic courses.

## Approach and implementation

The site processes segmentation and background effects in the browser and incorporates the interaction into course pages.

## Outcome

The project resulted in two software copyright registrations: 《基于 TensorFlow 在 360 度全景视频中进行人物分割系统 V1.0》 and 《全景课程 AI 教师模拟互动平台 V1.0》.
