Hello, I’m Kunrong Li. I study Internet of Things Engineering in the College of Computer Science and Fan Gongxiu Honors College at Beijing University of Technology. Through coursework and projects, I have become interested in using technology to address concrete problems: organizing complex information, helping people understand results, and enabling systems to act more effectively in their surroundings.

I have worked on a review tool for software approval materials and contributed to data processing and workflow improvements for a financial Q&A Agent, knowledge base, and report automation. My research experience includes multi-agent collaboration, organizing consumption data, and autonomous robot exploration in unknown indoor environments. Other projects have involved EEG analysis software and browser-based AI interaction for panoramic education. These areas differ, but each has helped me practice starting with a problem, understanding its data, designing an approach, and building something usable.

I created this site to keep an evolving record of my projects, learning, and exploration. The project pages describe the context, my contributions, and the outcomes available so far. The writing section is a place for future reflections and practical notes. As I continue learning, the site may also grow beyond AI-related topics.
