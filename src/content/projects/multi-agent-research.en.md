## Background

I participate in a research project using computing resources provided by Nanyang Technological University. The project studies MBTI personality trait inference from everyday consumption data.

## My role and contribution

I built a collaborative multi-agent workflow with Claude and designed multidimensional dataset standards across six consumption scenarios. I am responsible for collecting, cleaning, and standardizing data from multiple sources.

## Approach and implementation

The workflow coordinates agents across research tasks, while the dataset standards bring information from different sources and scenarios into a consistent form.

## Outcome

Two related papers have been submitted to AAAI. Their status is submitted.
