## Background

As a member of my university's RoboMaster algorithm team, I worked on robot navigation research focused on autonomous exploration in unknown indoor environments.

## My role and contribution

The research used a lightweight neural network to predict unobserved, occluded areas in a local map and drew on regularities in indoor structures to improve exploration paths. The target setting was mapping and exploration on resource-constrained edge devices.

## Approach and implementation

Predicting unknown parts of a local map supplies additional environmental cues for path planning while accounting for limited on-device resources.

## Outcome

I contributed to one IJCNN 2025 paper.
